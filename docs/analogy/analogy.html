Analogy Series — Index

Ben Um · March 2026

This series follows a single thread: how analogy emerges in cognition and in large language models, and how we might begin to glimpse the latent domain behind it. It moves from first principles—analogy as analog primitive, coordinate space as digital harness—through reflexive experiments that ask frontier models to describe what they cannot yet name, toward instrumentation: probing the KV cache, reducing descriptions to minimal kernels, and listening for coherence events. The arc is speculative and incremental, grounded in epistemic humility rather than solved ontology.
